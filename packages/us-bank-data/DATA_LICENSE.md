# Data attribution and reuse

Code is MIT. The bundled `2026-q2-r1` edition reproduces BanksOnSale's
public FDIC financial compilation and original metadata under CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Original observations are from FDIC BankFind Suite. No FDIC endorsement or
exclusive ownership of government observations is implied. Source definitions:
https://api.fdic.gov/banks/docs/

Suggested credit: **US Bank Data; BanksOnSale compilation and metadata, sourced
from FDIC BankFind Suite**, with edition ID, report date and dataset ID.
The package normalizes monetary values from USD thousands into USD exactly once.
The original public files and source responses retain their original units.

The 2026Q2 edition includes 4,313 institution financial records, including NC and
OI classes. It is not restricted to insured institutions or banks active today.
Identity labels and data revisions reflect the source at retrieval, not an
original-filing or reconstructed historical identity vintage. The original
manifest, scope, retrieval times and hashes are preserved in the bundle and
portable cohort manifests. This license does not cover private platform data.
